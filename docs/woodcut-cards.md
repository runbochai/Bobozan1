# Woodcut cards and apricot aura

The approved collection supplies all 61 skill illustrations and three folder covers. Basic skills use hands, equipment or energy rather than whole characters. The same face is used in the hand, played cards, details and permanent-skill selection.

Cost, effective skill level, folder count and localized title are live UI layers. They cover the sample lettering printed into the art, so free skills and endless progression cannot display stale numbers. Special and combined cards retain their gold holographic finish.

The approved eight-frame apricot effect is filled, with no center cutout. Its alpha layer can overlap neighbouring cards while the current card is drawn above its own aura. It does not receive clicks. The effect follows fan rotation, hover and scrolling, stops when unaffordable/locked/dead or reduced motion is enabled, and does not resize the tabletop. Multiple ready skills use lighter layers.

Hover lifts a card; one click plays it. Category and information buttons only browse.
