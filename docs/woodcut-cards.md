# Parchment cards and apricot aura

Cards use warm, aged parchment, a stitched brown border, and one large central woodcut. Charge has a brown lantern with a gold flame. Other skills retain their individual illustrations: an SVG crop removes the sample labels and a luminance-to-alpha filter turns the illustration into a brown ink impression while dropping its dark field. The original assets and mappings stay intact. Category covers use large attack, guard and crown symbols and show their skill count.

Cost, effective skill level, folder count and localized title are live UI layers. Small corner stamps show cost and skill level; the foot of offensive cards identifies their separate attack tier. Combo and Endless growth marks use the actual card data. Free skills show zero cost, large levels use compact text with the precise value in the accessible name, and special and combined cards retain a restrained gold holographic finish.

The same face is used in the hand, locked cards, played cards, details, permanent-skill selection, move manual and free sparring. Smaller teaching and reward illustrations use the same central woodcut on paper. Only presentation changes: level progression, tiers, free and temporary uses, disabled states and submission timing are unchanged.

The approved eight-frame apricot effect is filled, with no center cutout. Its alpha layer can overlap neighbouring cards while the current card is drawn above its own aura. It does not receive clicks. The effect follows fan rotation, hover and scrolling, stops when unaffordable/locked/dead or reduced motion is enabled, and does not resize the tabletop. Multiple ready skills use lighter layers.

Hover lifts a card; one click plays it. Category and information buttons only browse.
